1. Tomato Puree Setup (1/2):
Take a pan, and add the following:
- Oil (2/tbsp)
- Butter (2/tbsp)
- Cloves (5/pcs)
- Cardamom (2/pcs)

Mix in low flame

2. Tomato Puree Setup (2/2):
When aroma kicks in, add the following:
- Onions (2/sliced)
- Garlic (2/cloves)
- Ginger (1/inch)

[Mix well - Close lid and boil for 10 minutes until tomato softens].

Let the puree cool completely and grind it into smooth paste. Strain recommended.

3. Curry:
Add the following:
- Oil (2/tbsp)
- Butter (2/tbsp)
- Bay Leaf (1/pcs)
- Chilli (1/sliced)

[Mix until aromatic smell kicks-in].

Add:
- Onion (1/finely chopped)

[Mix well until onions turn golden brown].

4. Masala:
Add the following masala:
- Turmeric (1/2 tsp)
- Chilli Powder (1 tsp)
- Coriander Powder (3/4 tsp)
- Cumin Powder (1/4 tsp)
- Garam Masala (1/2 tsp)
- Salt (1 tsp)

[Mix well until aromatic smell kicks-in].

5. Mix-max Puree with base:
Now add the tomato puree paste to the pan and mix well.
Add water according to required consistency.

Add the following:
- Fresh Cream (2 tbsp)
- Kasuri Methi (1 tsp | Crushed)
- Paneer

[Close lid and let it simmer for about 5 minutes]
